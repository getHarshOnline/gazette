# Keep the observation with its context

Write what you noticed before deciding what it means. Include the place, conditions and any detail that could change your interpretation when you return to the note.

An observation becomes more useful when another person can understand how you reached it. Keep the original measurement, sketch or quotation beside the explanation instead of replacing it with a confident conclusion.

When you are ready to interpret the note, [[id:content:review-a-note|separate the observation from the claim]].
