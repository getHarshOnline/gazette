# Notes tooling

Field Notes stores each note's identity, title and slug in its Markdown frontmatter. Gazette derives the address from the slug, collection placement and publication routing. A tool that edits a note's title should change its display text while retaining the key used to recognize it, its slug and its reading relationships.

[Note identity and addresses](reading-notes.md#stable-note-identity) explains those three values. For the author-facing steps, use [Rename a note](https://field-notes.example/writing/rename-a-note/).
